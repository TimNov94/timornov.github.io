The Silence of Patagonia

A journey into the wind-swept wilderness of Torres del Paine, capturing the isolation and grand scale of South America's southern tip. Trekking through unpredictable weather, the landscape reveals frozen mountain spires, turquoise glacial lakes, and solitary guanacos grazing on open pampas.

Chasing Light in the Swiss Alps

High-altitude photography exploring changing seasons across alpine valleys, glaciated peaks, and quiet mountain lakes. From dawn light breaking over Eiger to golden dusk reflections near Zermatt, this collection documents nature's enduring power and subtle shifts.

Faces & Traditions of New Zealand

Documenting local artisans, coastal communities, and indigenous heritage along the South Island's rugged coast. Beyond stunning scenery lies deep human connection—people living in harmony with nature and preserving ancestral traditions for future generations.
