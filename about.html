Behind the Lens

My name is Timor Novoselsky. For years, photography has been my way of connecting with the wildest corners of our planet. From capturing rare alpine wildlife to documenting the quiet rhythms of remote mountain communities, my passion lies in authentic storytelling.

I am currently preparing for an exciting new chapter: moving to Bern, Switzerland. This relocation marks both a personal and creative milestone, bringing me closer to the heart of the European Alps.

Whether working in extreme weather conditions or waiting hours for a single frame, I approach every project with patience, respect for nature, and an eye for minimalist beauty.
