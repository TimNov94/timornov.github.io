About Timor

I am Timor, a travel, landscape, and wildlife photographer driven by an endless curiosity for remote places and authentic cultural narratives. For me, photography is an act of quiet observation—capturing fleeting light, untouched wilderness, and human connection in its rawest form.
My work reflects a deep reverence for nature, whether navigating the rugged wind-swept steppes of Patagonia, tracking wildlife across alpine ridges, or documenting heritage communities in New Zealand. As I prepare for my move to Bern, Switzerland, I look forward to exploring the heart of Europe's alpine landscapes and sharing new visual stories.
